# App Store Listings Guide (Task 40)

## Overview

This guide helps you prepare compelling app store listings for both Google Play Store and Apple App Store.

## App Information

### App Name
**Vietnamese Food Detection** or **VN Food Detector**

Keep it under 30 characters for better display.

### Short Description (Google Play - 80 characters)
```
Detect Vietnamese dishes instantly with AI. Get nutrition info in seconds!
```

### Subtitle (App Store - 30 characters)
```
AI-Powered Food Recognition
```

## Screenshots

### Requirements

**Google Play**:
- Minimum: 2 screenshots
- Recommended: 5-8 screenshots
- Size: 1080 x 1920 (portrait) or 1920 x 1080 (landscape)
- Format: PNG or JPEG

**App Store**:
- Minimum: 3 screenshots per device size
- Recommended: 5-8 screenshots
- Sizes:
  - 6.5" Display: 1284 x 2778 (iPhone 14 Pro Max)
  - 5.5" Display: 1242 x 2208 (iPhone 8 Plus)
- Format: PNG or JPEG

### Screenshot Ideas

1. **Home Screen**
   - Title: "Welcome to VN Food Detection"
   - Show: Clean home screen with camera and gallery buttons
   - Highlight: Simple, intuitive interface

2. **Camera Capture**
   - Title: "Capture Any Vietnamese Dish"
   - Show: Camera view with food in frame
   - Highlight: Easy photo capture

3. **Detection Results**
   - Title: "Instant AI Recognition"
   - Show: Image with bounding boxes and dish names
   - Highlight: Fast, accurate detection

4. **Nutrition Information**
   - Title: "Detailed Nutrition Data"
   - Show: Nutrition card with calories, protein, carbs, fat
   - Highlight: Complete nutritional breakdown

5. **Multiple Dishes**
   - Title: "Detect Multiple Items"
   - Show: Image with multiple dishes detected
   - Highlight: Handles complex meals

6. **Share Results**
   - Title: "Share with Friends"
   - Show: Share dialog
   - Highlight: Easy sharing

7. **Supported Dishes**
   - Title: "30+ Vietnamese Dishes"
   - Show: Grid of supported dishes
   - Highlight: Wide variety

8. **High Accuracy**
   - Title: "97%+ Accuracy"
   - Show: Confidence scores
   - Highlight: Reliable results

## App Icon

### Requirements

**Google Play**:
- Size: 512 x 512 pixels
- Format: PNG (32-bit)
- No transparency

**App Store**:
- Size: 1024 x 1024 pixels
- Format: PNG
- No transparency
- No alpha channel

### Design Guidelines

- Simple and recognizable
- Works at small sizes
- Represents Vietnamese food
- Matches app theme
- No text (or minimal)

### Icon Ideas

1. **Bowl of Phở**

## Privacy Policy

### Required Information

Your privacy policy must cover:
- What data is collected
- How data is used
- How data is stored
- Third-party services
- User rights
- Contact information

### Template

```markdown
# Privacy Policy for Vietnamese Food Detection

Last updated: [Date]

## Data Collection

We collect minimal data:
- Photos: Processed for detection, not stored
- API requests: Logged for debugging, deleted after 30 days
- No personal information collected
- No user accounts required

## Data Usage

- Photos are sent to our server for AI processing
- Results are returned to your device
- No data is sold to third parties
- No advertising or tracking

## Data Storage

- Photos are not stored on our servers
- Detection results are not saved
- API logs are deleted after 30 days

## Third-Party Services

We use:
- Railway.app for hosting
- No analytics services
- No advertising networks

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Use the app without providing personal data
- Request data deletion (contact us)
- Opt out of any data collection

## Contact

For privacy concerns:
Email: [your-email]
Website: [your-website]

## Changes

We may update this policy. Check this page for updates.
```

## Launch Strategy

### Soft Launch
1. Release in one country first (e.g., Vietnam)
2. Gather feedback
3. Fix issues
4. Expand to more countries

### Full Launch
1. Release worldwide
2. Monitor reviews
3. Respond to feedback
3. Update regularly

## Post-Launch

### Monitor
- Download numbers
- User reviews
- Crash reports
- Support requests

### Respond
- Reply to reviews (especially negative ones)
- Fix bugs quickly
- Add requested features
- Update regularly

### Promote
